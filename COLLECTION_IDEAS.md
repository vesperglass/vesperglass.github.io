# Vesperglass collectible image ideas

This is an internal planning document for future six-image collectible series. Every series must connect specifically to Vesperglass through a place, object, event, or established world detail. Generated designs and their serial numbers remain editorial interpretations unless a source establishes the objects; do not present a website collection as a recovered in-world inventory. Prefer a science-fiction visual treatment where it suits the subject.

The current first collection, **Vesperglass Workshop Tools**, is already in progress and is not ranked here.

## Ranking criteria

Rate each concept from 1 to 5 on six criteria. Scores are a planning judgment, not a measured outcome.

| Criterion | Weight | A high rating means… |
|---|---:|---|
| Vesperglass connection (V) | 30% | The concept draws directly on a named Vesperglass place, object, event, or established detail. |
| Science-fiction visual potential (SF) | 20% | It supports striking, distinctive futuristic or fantastical imagery. |
| Six-image variety (6) | 20% | It naturally yields six distinct subjects or views without repetition. |
| Set cohesion (C) | 15% | The images can share one style and image type while reading as a collectible set. |
| Lore fit (L) | 10% | It can be framed clearly without turning interpretation into established fact. |
| Portfolio diversity (D) | 5% | It adds a different subject or visual format to the other series under consideration. |

For ratings 1–5, calculate the score out of 100 as:

`V × 6 + SF × 4 + 6 × 4 + C × 3 + L × 2 + D`

The rating column below uses `V/SF/6/C/L/D`. Rank by total score. Ties break by Vesperglass connection, lore fit, six-image variety, then title alphabetically. Concepts with V below 3 remain listed but are marked **Hold** until they have a stronger Vesperglass anchor.
