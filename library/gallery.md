## Illustrated record and source notes

This register brings together the illustrations and diagrams that accompany Vesperglass accounts: named-place views, Atlas panels, plans, and figure studies. A card's **reading status** distinguishes an account-based view from an interpretation, an editorial drawing, or a picture whose source remains unassigned. Its **plate kind** describes the form of the image. These labels help a reader find a view and judge its limits; a proposed illustration does not establish a place's measured shape, construction, or furnishings. Search by subject or select several topic and plate-kind labels to narrow the register.

<h3 id="asset-kind-filters">Plate kinds · forms of illustration</h3>

Choose one plate kind to narrow the register, then combine it with topic labels. A kind distinguishes a place view from a plan, a field panel, or a sequence of poses; reading status separately records whether its details are interpreted, diagrammed, or source-unassigned. Neither label establishes the identity or history of a depicted object.

The register groups views as **Folio illustrations**, **Atlas place illustrations**, **Atlas background fields**, **Plans and diagrams**, **Figure studies**, or **Other illustrations**. The group appears on each card and can be found by name as well as by selecting a filter. Star Archive plates are set aside initially and can be included with the Archive control; the available kinds and topic labels adjust to the chosen set.

### Atlas destination views · eight named places

These eight Atlas views are paired with named destinations and linked to their corresponding accounts below. The descriptions name visible features so each place can be recognized in the register. The illustrations are interpretive views: their roofs, terraces, windows, gardens, and platform outlines are not measured records, and a resemblance to a folio plate does not confirm that the two pictures show one structure.

| Named destination | What the Atlas view depicts | Reading limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Star Archive]({{ '/library/star-archive/' | relative_url }}) | A round, many-windowed hall with a broad terrace and glazed dome. | Its pictured exterior and ornament are not an established architectural survey. |
| [Threshold House]({{ '/library/threshold-house/' | relative_url }}) | A pale gabled house beside an attached glass conservatory. | Its form does not establish the House's purpose, plan, or inhabitants. |
| [Vesperglass Workshop]({{ '/library/workshop/' | relative_url }}) | A broad hall with glazed roofing, copper pipes, and a bright interior. | Its equipment, exterior, and scale remain illustrative. |
| [Observatory Logbook]({{ '/library/observatory/' | relative_url }}) | A narrow tower with a split copper dome and outward-facing telescope. | The tower and telescope arrangement are not described in the surviving account. |
| [Compass Garden]({{ '/library/compass-garden/' | relative_url }}) | A domed glasshouse within curved flower beds and a central compass rose. | The plants, paths, and layout do not establish species, cultivation, scale, or bearing. |
| [Tideglass Foundry]({{ '/library/tideglass-foundry/' | relative_url }}) | A glazed industrial hall with chimney, pipes, and a lit furnace. | The image does not verify the Foundry's exterior or its process equipment. |
| [The Vesperglass Gate]({{ '/library/vesperglass-gate/' | relative_url }}) | A bright blue opening held within a tall ring at the end of a platform. | The illustration does not specify the ring's dimensions, material, or construction. |
| [Vesperglass Train Dock]({{ '/library/train-dock/' | relative_url }}) | A long platform lined with lamps, planters, and a covered passage. | The view gives no surveyed berth, approach line, or measured route. |

The descriptions on each image card repeat these visible features for readers using a screen reader. The Atlas's field of small lights is a separate background view, not a chart of named stars; the destination illustrations help recognize accounts but do not locate or measure their buildings.

| Image group | Plate kind | Reading status | What the named illustration supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Eight named destination views** | Atlas place illustrations | Interpretation | Each view accompanies the named account linked in the destination register. | A surveyed island shape, scale, orientation, or exact correspondence with another illustration. |
| **Repeating star field** | Atlas background fields | Atlas background | A field of small lights appears behind the Atlas place views. | Named stars, celestial measurements, a verified sky chart, or a route. |
| **Older orbital and landmark drawings** | Other illustrations | Source unassigned | The drawings can be compared visually and are kept distinct from the eight named views. | Their origin, intended reading, relation to each other, or correspondence with a named celestial body or place. |

Plate kind and reading status answer different questions: an Atlas destination view is both a place illustration and an interpretation. Older orbital and landmark drawings remain source-unassigned because no account or current Atlas view identifies their subject. The <a href="{{ '/explore/' | relative_url }}">Vesperglass Atlas</a> lets readers consult named locations; the <a href="{{ '/library/vesperglass-gate/' | relative_url }}">Gate account</a> and <a href="{{ '/library/train-dock/' | relative_url }}">Train Dock account</a> preserve the written descriptions that accompany two of those views.
