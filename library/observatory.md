## The place in the atlas

Observatory Logbook is the atlas’s named observatory destination. The name joins a place of looking with a record of what is seen, but no particular observation, archive or resident observer has yet been established in the setting.

## An architectural reading

This folio imagines a room where instruments and written notes sit side by side. The orrery offers a way to compare positions, while the logbook keeps observations in a form that can be revisited. These are architectural and furnishing decisions for the illustration, not features or measurements in the current map.

## From an imagined logbook

The short entry below is an interpretive example of how a keeper might separate an observation from a conclusion. It is invented for this folio and is not a reading from the Galaxy viewer.

<aside class="field-note"><span class="field-note-mark" aria-hidden="true">✳</span><p><strong>Sample entry · no date recorded</strong><br><em>At the rim of the blue field, one point held its color through three turns of the sighting tube. I have marked the position for a second watch. The light may be steady; the instrument may be drifting. Leave the cause open until another observer can compare it.</em></p></aside>

The note records what the observer thinks they saw, what should be checked next, and which explanation remains uncertain. Its language and procedure are proposals, not established Observatory practice.
