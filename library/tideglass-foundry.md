## The place in the atlas

Tideglass Foundry is the Vesperglass platform tied to the universe’s starshard lore. In the recorded account, gathered starshards are cleaned, ground between mirrored plates and sifted into a fine powder. The dust is then tempered in the Gate’s low resonance to become stardust for the train’s starwell.

## An architectural reading

The room and instruments on these plates expand that process into an architectural scene. The imagined mill is small and deliberate: the work depends on reading a shard’s internal planes and preserving a clean, even powder. No active refining station or resource inventory is implemented in the current viewer.
