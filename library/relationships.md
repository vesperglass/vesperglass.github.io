## Connections, with their limits

This index gathers relationships recorded across the Encyclopedia. It follows the movement of starshards, the places that shape a crossing, and the limits of what the surviving accounts tell us.

| Connection | Recorded account | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starshards → Tideglass Foundry | Starshards gathered from distant bodies are brought to Vesperglass and prepared at the Foundry. | The accounts name no specific deposit, crew, or journey by which a shard arrived. |
| Tideglass Foundry → stardust → train starwell | Foundry work cleans, grinds, sifts, and tempers starshards into stardust; the train carries it in a sealed starwell. | Exact measures, equipment dimensions, and the amount needed for a crossing are unrecorded. |
| Train Dock ↔ Vesperglass Gate | The Dock and the Gate stand on separate platforms; the Gate is the train's passage between worlds. | The accounts establish no track or fixed route between the two platforms. |
| Train Dock → free flight | The train approaches the Dock, berths alongside it, departs, and returns to open flight. | No timetable, named arrival, or dated departure is recorded here. |
| Vesperglass Gate → distant worlds | The train passes through the Gate to travel between worlds. | No destination, duration, or passenger list is attached to a particular crossing. |
| Vesperglass Workshop → the wider archipelago | The Workshop is Vesperglass's central home and the place from which the other platforms are understood. | The accounts do not describe walkable paths or a fixed route among every platform. |

### How to read the distinctions

These entries distinguish what an account states from details it leaves open. A link between folios is a reading aid; it does not add a route or journey beyond the relationships recorded above.

Architectural readings and proposed customs elsewhere in the Encyclopedia offer possible interpretations. They do not supply missing dimensions, schedules, or geographic routes.
