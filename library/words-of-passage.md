## Terms used in accounts and atlas views

Several terms describe different parts of a train's passage. Some belong to the accounts of travel; one names a path suggested by the train's movement at its berth. This cross-reference gathers them without making the terms into an operating sequence or adding a route between the Gate and the Train Dock.

<dl class="folio-glossary">
  <div><dt>Stardust · account</dt><dd><p>Fine powder made by cleaning, grinding, sifting, and tempering starshards at Tideglass Foundry. Accounts describe it as the material carried for a crossing.</p><p><strong>Still open:</strong> No measure, vessel capacity, or quantity for a particular journey is recorded.</p></dd></div>
  <div><dt>Starwell · account</dt><dd><p>The locomotive's sealed glass chamber. Stardust gathers there into the pulse that lets the train lean against distance and find the Gate's far side.</p><p><strong>Still open:</strong> The chamber's dimensions and engineering are not specified.</p></dd></div>
  <div><dt>Gate bloom · account</dt><dd><p>The bright light that gathers around the train as the Gate passage takes shape, briefly hiding the locomotive before the bloom opens outward again at arrival.</p><p><strong>Still open:</strong> The accounts assign no schedule, duration, or destination to a particular crossing.</p></dd></div>
  <div><dt>Guide path · atlas view</dt><dd><p>At the Train Dock, the atlas shows the locomotive following an unseen alignment as it approaches, berths alongside the platform, and departs. “Guide path” names that reading of the displayed movement.</p><p><strong>Still open:</strong> The path is not drawn as rails, and it does not establish a fixed route between the separate Dock and Gate platforms.</p></dd></div>
</dl>

### Where the terms meet

The accounts connect stardust to the starwell and the starwell to passage through the Gate. The Gate bloom describes the light around that crossing. The guide path belongs to the separate view of the train aligning with its berth at the Dock. These descriptions can be read together, but they do not make the Dock's alignment a track to the Gate or establish the details of any particular journey.

For the material and its place in the locomotive, see [Starshards & the Gate Train]({{ '/library/starshards/' | relative_url }}) and [Tideglass Foundry]({{ '/library/tideglass-foundry/' | relative_url }}). For the light and the berth, see [The Vesperglass Gate]({{ '/library/vesperglass-gate/' | relative_url }}) and [Vesperglass Train Dock]({{ '/library/train-dock/' | relative_url }}).
