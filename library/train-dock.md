## The place in the atlas

Vesperglass Train Dock is a platform separate from the Vesperglass Gate. It gives the locomotive a place to park alongside the station edge, leaving the platform itself clear. In the atlas animation, the train approaches in a broad curve, aligns with an invisible docking path, travels along the berth and departs through the path before returning to free flight.

## An architectural reading

The plates show a straight open edge and an alongside berth, with no tracks. The route diagram is a conceptual explanation of the visual docking behavior, not a claim that the rendered platform contains rails. The looping train animation is a presentation feature; persistent train operations are not yet part of the simulation.
