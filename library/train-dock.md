## The place in the atlas

Vesperglass Train Dock is a platform separate from the Vesperglass Gate. It gives the locomotive a place to berth alongside the station edge, leaving the platform itself clear. The train approaches in a broad curve, aligns with a guide path, travels alongside the berth and departs through the same passage before returning to open flight.

## An architectural reading

The plates show a straight open edge and an alongside berth, with no tracks. The approach and departure path is understood from the train's movement; the Dock itself has no recorded rails.
