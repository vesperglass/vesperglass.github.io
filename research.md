## Follow a question across disciplines

Some questions begin with a star pattern; others with a small piece of hardware. These essays move between what can be observed, what the historical record says, and what a present-day reader might make of it.

The sources are linked so you can retrace the path. Cultural traditions are named in their own contexts, and the essays distinguish documented history from interpretation.
