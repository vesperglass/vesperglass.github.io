## The habits behind the work

<div class="principle-list">
  <div class="principle"><span>01</span><div><h2>Begin with a real question</h2><p>Find out who needs the work and what would make it useful. Stay curious long enough to notice a better question.</p></div></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>02</span><div><h2>Make the reasoning visible</h2><p>Keep sources, assumptions, and limits close to the result. Clear context lets another person check the work and carry it forward.</p></div></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>03</span><div><h2>Prefer useful over impressive</h2><p>Choose the smallest shape that helps someone. Add polish where it improves understanding, not just for its own sake.</p></div></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>04</span><div><h2>Leave a trail</h2><p>Write down how to use a project and where its important information came from. A good map is part of the thing being built.</p></div></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>05</span><div><h2>Revise with the evidence</h2><p>Make it easy to improve the answer. When new information arrives, update the work and explain what changed.</p></div></div>
</div>
